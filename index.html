About Me

I'm a software engineer at BeamNG GmbH, working on BeamNG.tech — the company's engineering simulation platform for autonomous driving and ADAS development. I integrate the simulator with ROS/ROS2, MATLAB and Simulink, build the API bridges that make real-time automation possible, and QA across C++, Python and Lua.

Before BeamNG I built autonomous delivery and navigation systems on robotics teams in Malaysia and Singapore, after graduating in Mechatronics Engineering. I'm based in Istanbul, Turkey, and I'm always up for anything involving robotics, simulation or automation.
